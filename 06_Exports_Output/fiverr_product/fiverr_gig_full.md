## FAQ

**Q: Will I lose any data?**
A: Never. I keep a backup of your original file and provide a cleaning log so you can see exactly what changed.

**Q: Can you handle sensitive data?**
A: Yes. Files are processed locally and deleted after delivery. I don't store or share your data.

**Q: What if I need changes after delivery?**
A: Free revisions included. Just tell me what needs adjusting.

**Q: Do you support non-English data?**
A: Yes! I handle multi-language data including Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and more.

**Q: Can you automate recurring cleaning tasks?**
A: Yes! For ongoing projects, I can set up automated pipelines. Message me for custom pricing.
